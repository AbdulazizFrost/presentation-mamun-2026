What Is Time Management?

Vaqtni boshqarish nima?

Time management is the skill of planning your time and spending it on the things that really matter.

Slide 03 • Benefits

Why Is It Important for Students?

Nega bu talabalar uchun muhim?

Students have lectures, homework, exams, part-time jobs and a personal life. Good time management helps to do everything without stress.

Talabalarda darslar, uy vazifalari, imtihonlar, ish va shaxsiy hayot bor. Vaqtni to‘g‘ri boshqarish hammasiga stresssiz ulgurishga yordam beradi.

Less stress

Kamroq stress

No last-minute panic before deadlines.

Better grades

Yaxshiroq baholar

Regular study instead of cramming.

Bir kecha emas, muntazam o‘qish.

More free time

Ko‘proq bo‘sh vaqt

Time for friends, sport and hobbies.

Do‘stlar, sport va sevimli mashg‘ulotlarga vaqt.

Healthy sleep

Sog‘lom uyqu
